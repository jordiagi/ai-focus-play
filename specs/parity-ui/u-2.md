# U-2 — Jersey numbers only, never invented names

## Problem
**Real Veo never displays a player name anywhere** — jersey numbers only — and renders
`Player ` with a *blank* number for players it could not resolve. Our clone invents
names: "Eric Jordi", "Diego Morales", "Julian Vance", "Alex Reed". Those are fabricated
identities presented as data.

Separately, `PlayerMomentsBar.tsx:17-19` hardcodes **17 jersey literals**
(`'2','4','6',...`) instead of deriving them from `match.lineup`. Jerseys 26 and 30
appear there but are not in the roster, and seeded `GK`/`1`/`44` are handled
inconsistently.

## Files OWNED
- `frontend/src/components/PlayerMomentsBar.tsx`
- `backend/src/storage/repository.py`
- `backend/tests/unit/test_analytics_consistency.py`

## Files READ (do not edit)
`backend/src/domain/models/match.py`, `frontend/src/components/Sidebar/SidebarTabs.tsx`,
`frontend/src/types/index.ts`

## Contract
- Derive the jersey bar from `match.lineup`, never from a literal list. If `lineup` is
  empty, render an **empty state**, not invented jerseys.
- Where a jersey is unknown, render Veo's convention — the label with a blank number —
  rather than omitting the player or inventing a number.
- Remove invented player names from the demo seed. A demo may be synthetic, but it must
  not assert identities that do not exist. Jersey + position is enough.
- `player_name` may remain in the model (another package reads it); it must simply not be
  populated with fiction, and the UI must not display a fabricated one.
- Keep the seed internally consistent — probe `d5` must still pass.
